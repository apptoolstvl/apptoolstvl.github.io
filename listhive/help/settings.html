Settings

The Settings tab holds the help link, import and QR codes, appearance, backups, the app lock, storage, your data and ads.

Share, help and Trends

  • Share ListHive: recommend the app to a friend.
  • Help & User Guide: opens this guide.
  • Trends: see below.
  • Import and Create QR Code: see Import, CSV & QR.

Backups have their own page.

Appearance: size, theme and colours

  • List Tile Size: Small, Normal or Large text and tiles on the My Lists and Libraries screens.
  • Theme: Light, Dark, or System to follow your phone.
  • Color Scheme: each circle is a pair of colours, one for My Lists and one for Libraries. The default is Indigo & Cyan. The last swatch is Custom: tap either dot to choose your own colours, then Use my custom colours.

Text size in the player and item screens is separate: use the small and large A buttons there.

App Lock: PIN and fingerprint

Turn on App Lock to require a PIN whenever ListHive opens.

  1. Switch on App Lock.
  2. Create a PIN of 4 to 6 digits, then enter it again to confirm.
  3. If your phone has a fingerprint or face unlock, ListHive offers to use it too (Enable Biometrics).

Once it's on, you also get:

  • Use Biometrics: fingerprint or face as the main way in, with your PIN as backup.
  • Auto-lock After: how soon ListHive locks again once you leave it: straight away, after a short time in the background (5 minutes unless you change it), or only when the app is restarted.
  • Change PIN and Lock Now.

To turn the lock off, switch off App Lock and enter your PIN.

The app lock protects ListHive on your phone. It doesn't encrypt backups: set a backup password for that.

Favourites

Tap the ☆ on any list, group or library to make it a favourite. The Faves button at the right of the bottom bar (and the ★ in the header of most screens) opens Open Favourite, where your favourites are grouped under My Lists, Groups and Libraries. Tap one to jump straight there.

Trends

Settings → Trends shows how you use your libraries:

  • Most Used: the library items played most often. In our kitchen, the curry.
  • Recent: what you opened last.
  • Completion: how far through each list and group you are.

Your data

DATABASE counts your libraries, library items, lists, groups and list items.

DATA MANAGEMENT has two clean-up buttons:

  • Clear Sample Data: removes the example lists (their names start with [Sample]) that come with a new install.
  • Clear All Data: deletes everything, permanently. Make a backup first.

See also Media storage and freeing space.

Ads and Remove Ads

ListHive is free and shows a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. What data that involves is explained in the privacy policy. If you're in a region that requires it, you'll be asked for consent first.

To get rid of the ads for good, tap Remove Ads in the PREMIUM section. It's a one-time purchase through Google Play, with no subscription. Afterwards the section says Ads Removed – Thank you for your support!

New phone, or reinstalled the app? Tap Restore Purchase. It checks your Google Play account and removes the ads again at no cost.

Updates and version

Check for Updates opens ListHive on Google Play. When a new version is out, ListHive may also show Update Available when you open it. Tap Update to go to the store, or Later.

ABOUT shows your App Version, e.g. 1.0.35 (35). Please include it if you email us about a problem.
