Sharing

Sharing

Send a library, a list, a group, a single item or a selection to someone else, in whatever form suits them. You can also save links, text and photos into ListHive straight from other apps.

What you can share

Look for the share icon at the top of a library, a list, a group or an item, or select several items and tap Share. The sheet that opens always offers the same four choices:

ChoiceBest for
Export as FileAnother ListHive user who wants the real thing: every field, photo and note, ready to import.
Share as TextAnyone at all: a readable summary for a chat or email.
Share as ImageA tidy picture of the list for a chat or social post.
Share Media FilesJust the original photos, videos, audio and documents.

Export as File (.listhive)

Export as File packs everything into a single .listhive file that the other person opens in ListHive. See Importing a .listhive file.

  • Leave Include media files ticked to send the photos, videos, audio and files too. Untick it for a much smaller file that holds only the text.
  • Sharing a list also includes the library items it uses, so the recipient doesn't need your libraries first.
  • Large libraries with many photos can take a while to pack.
Exported files are not encrypted

Anyone who gets the file can read it. For a private copy of all your data, use an encrypted backup instead.

The recipient needs ListHive installed. You can send them the app with Settings → Share ListHive.

Share as Text

Creates a formatted summary, with the list name, section headings, each item and its fields, and sends it to any app that takes text. Links stay tappable in most chat apps. Photos and other media aren't included; use Share Media Files for those.

To leave some fields out (for example, the Method when you share a party menu), turn off their Share box in field visibility.

Share as Image

Draws the list as a clean card. Scroll the preview to check it, then tap Share Image. The same field visibility settings decide which fields appear.

Share Media Files

Sends the original photos, videos, audio recordings and documents from what you're sharing (the number is shown on the button), without any ListHive text. If some files can't be sent, ListHive tells you how many were skipped.

Save things from other apps

Found a recipe online? Share it to ListHive. In your browser, gallery or almost any app, tap Share and pick ListHive. The Save Shared Content screen opens with what you shared, and asks WHERE TO SAVE?

  • Add Link: as a link item in a list (and group).
  • Add Note: as a note in a list.
  • Add Media: shared photos, videos or files as items in a list.
  • Item Field: into a field of an existing library item, such as the Source of a recipe.
  • Library Field: as a new library item. You can even create the library on the spot.

For a link, choose the list, check the URL and TITLE (ListHive fills in the page title for you), then tap Save. You'll see Saved – Content saved to ListHive.

Shared links often come with a picture from the page. Tap it to keep it with the link.

Recommend ListHive

Settings → Share ListHive sends a friend a short message with a link to the app on Google Play.
